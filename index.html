We're not the first ones to love this place. We won't be the last. Our job is just to be good to it during our turn.

Jill Craft  ·  Prissac, France
c.
1000

It was already old
when America was founded.

Jill noticed the chapel first. It sits in the courtyard, small and stone and quiet, the way things get when they've been standing long enough that nobody bothers to explain them anymore.

The records go back centuries. Before that, it gets harder to say. What we know is that people have been living on this hill for a very long time, and that the walls they built are still here.

We're still finding things. There's a pond on the property that dried up long before we got here. The water is gone but the little stone bridge is still standing, crossing nothing, patient as everything else on this land.

We're Jill and Jim. We didn't come to rescue this place. We came to take care of it for a while.
